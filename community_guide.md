# Pixel Harbor Community Guide

This is fictional information for the learning project.

## Events

- Community Night happens every Friday at 7:00 PM UTC in the `#party-lobby` voice channel. It is casual and does not require sign-up.
- Monthly tournament sign-up opens in `#tournament-desk`. The next date is not listed in this guide because the schedule can change. If a member asks for it, say a moderator in `#tournament-desk` can confirm the date. Do not ask the member to provide the date.

## Community support

- For login problems, members should use the game's official account recovery or support process. Never ask members to share passwords, security codes, email addresses, or other private account details in Discord.
- For bug reports, ask for the platform, game version, what happened, and the steps before the issue. Do not promise a fix or an investigation.

## Community guidelines

- Be respectful. Harassment, slurs, and sharing another person's private information are not allowed.
- When an answer or decision is not covered by this guide, a human moderator should confirm it.
